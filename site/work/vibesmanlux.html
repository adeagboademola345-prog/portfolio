Tailoring, with a sharper edge.

A bespoke menswear studio in Lagos. The site is a brand piece — photography, story, and a way to start a commission — rather than a catalogue.

01 — The idea

Trust before cloth.

Bespoke tailoring is sold on confidence: the customer needs to believe the studio can cut for them before they discuss a price. The site leads with the work — full-bleed photography and a short statement of the studio’s position — and keeps contact one screen away.

The pace is deliberate. Large images, few words, and no carousel fighting the customer for control.

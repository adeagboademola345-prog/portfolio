01 — The idea

The photographs do the selling.

The shop had a name and product photographs, not a website. The build keeps the photographs large, the categories short, and the path to an order visible from the first screen — because most customers will arrive on a phone and decide in seconds.

Nothing in the layout competes with the product: a warm, quiet frame, clear prices, and one obvious action per screen.

Money lessons start early.

A banking app for children aged 4 to 16, with a parent’s dashboard behind it. The child gets a balance they understand; the parent gets control without hovering.

Role
Product design, UI
Platform
Mobile
Scope
Concept prototype
Tools
Figma

01 — The idea

One account, sixteen years.

Most children’s finance apps are either toys that stop teaching after a week, or adult banking apps with rounded corners. Sprout is built to hold a child from their first naira to their first job — the language and the limits change, the account does not.

A parent sees the account from above: what went in, what went out, and what the child is saving for. The child sees only what is theirs.

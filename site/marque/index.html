BUYING

The paper trail comes with the car.

  • 120-point inspection

    An independent mechanic checks the engine, gearbox, suspension, electrics and bodywork. The report, with its date, is on the car page before you pay anything.

  • Duty status, stated plainly

    “Duty paid” sits under the name, and the customs papers are verified as part of the inspection.

  • A named, rated dealer

    Every car shows who is selling it. Message them, book a viewing, or make an offer.

  • Offer, and finance it

    Offer the asking price or a few percent under. Turn on “Finance this car” for an indicative monthly figure, subject to approval.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions people ask first.

How does the 120-point inspection work?

An independent mechanic checks the engine, gearbox, suspension, electrics and bodywork, and we verify the customs papers. You get the full report before you pay anything.

Can I rent without a chauffeur?

Yes. Many cars offer self-drive with a verified driver’s licence and a refundable deposit. Cars marked “With chauffeur” come with a vetted driver.

How does financing work?

Turn on “Finance this car” when you make an offer. Our partner banks send an indicative monthly figure, then confirm after review.

What if I need to cancel a rental?

Cancel free up to 48 hours before pick-up. After that, the first day is charged and the rest refunded.
